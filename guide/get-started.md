<h1>Your First Site</h1>
<p class="lede">Create a project, place your first parts, and see your page in a browser.</p>

## Create a project

Open Foundry and choose **File → New**. A new project starts with a single Home page and Foundry's standard design framework.

## Place your first part

Drag a part — try **Heading** — from the Parts panel onto the page. Select it on the canvas and use the Inspector on the right to change its text and style.

## Preview in your browser

Click **Preview** in the toolbar. Foundry serves your site locally and opens it in your default browser; edits appear when you refresh.
